Why It Matters

The wing group took the hit. Ingram's Achilles and Strus's foot land on the same spot on the roster before a regular-season game has counted, which leaves Derrick Jones Jr., Gradey Dick and rookie Keaton Wagler as the depth to watch.

Ingram came over in the Kawhi Leonard trade, so his recovery is the biggest swing factor in how that deal looks. Strus's four-week check-in lands right around the Nov 1 game at Golden State.
