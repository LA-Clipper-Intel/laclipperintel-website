Why It Matters

The wing group took the hit. Ingram's Achilles kept him out while Strus's foot cost him the start of the season, which left Derrick Jones Jr., Gradey Dick and rookie Keaton Wagler as the depth to watch.

Ingram came over in the Kawhi Leonard trade, so his recovery is the biggest swing factor in how that deal looks. Strus has been back since Nov 2, which helps the wing rotation, but with Ingram and Beal both still out ten games in, the Clippers are short two of their offseason additions.
