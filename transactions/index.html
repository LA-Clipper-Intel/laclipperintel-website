Latest Moves

Newest first
  • Oct 17

    Clippers waive Exhibit 10 players Blake Wesley, Nathan Mensah, Miye Oni and Jahmyl Telfort, and re-sign Cam Christie to a two-way contract, per Hoops Rumors. The roster is set at 15 standard and 3 two-way deals ahead of the Oct 21 opener vs. the Kings. The four waived players are eligible to join the San Diego G League affiliate.

  • Oct 14

    Clippers waive guard Cam Christie, per beat writer Tomer Azarly, to get to the 15-man limit. He cleared waivers and re-signed on a two-way deal Oct 17 (see above).

  • Oct 12

    Clippers waive guard Yuki Kawamura, who was on an Exhibit 10 contract. Coach Ty Lue said it is not over for Yuki yet.

  • Oct 8

    Clippers sign free-agent wing Jahmyl Telfort to an Exhibit 10 contract. Fills the open spot on the 21-man preseason roster.

  • Oct 6

    Clippers waive rookie guard Fletcher Loyer. He was on a non-guaranteed Exhibit 10 deal.

  • Oct 4

    Max Strus suffers a partial plantar fascia tear in the preseason opener. Reevaluated in four weeks. See the injury report.

  • Sep 28

    Media Day at Intuit Dome. Brandon Ingram confirmed to have a partially torn Achilles. Training camp in Hawaii runs Sept 29 to Oct 3.

  • Sep 27

    Lawrence Frank suspended six months by the NBA. Trent Redden is interim president of basketball operations and Mark Hughes handles day-to-day GM duties.

  • Sep 27

    Guards Hunter Sallis and Jason Preston sign Exhibit 10 deals, are waived and assigned to the San Diego G League affiliate.

  • Sep 27

    Clippers sign guard Blake Wesley to a one-year deal for camp backcourt depth. Guarantee terms are not yet confirmed by reporting.

Offseason Moves

Summer 2026
  • Trade

    Kawhi Leonard traded to the Toronto Raptors for Brandon Ingram, Gradey Dick, two unprotected first-round picks, a first-round pick swap and two second-round picks.

  • Signing

    Rui Hachimura signs for 2 years, $28M.

  • Signing

    Bradley Beal signs for 2 years, $13.2M using Non-Bird rights.

  • Trade

    Max Strus acquired via trade. Johni Broome, acquired in the same trade, is waived.

  • Waiver

    Two-way center Jamarion Sharp waived.

  • Roster

    Clippers sat at 16 guaranteed deals heading into training camp. That is now 15 standard deals after the Oct 17 cuts.

Exact dates for the summer moves are not listed here. The cap math behind them is on the cap sheet page.

Discipline & Front Office

NBA discipline

$30M fine, five first-round picks stripped

The NBA's investigation into Kawhi Leonard's compensation is closed. The organization was fined $30M and lost 5 first-round picks. Kawhi was personally fined for improper benefits.

Suspension

Lawrence Frank: six months

Suspended for signing off on Kawhi Leonard's impermissible endorsement arrangements. GM Trent Redden is interim president of basketball operations.

Ownership

Steve Ballmer: one year

Suspended one year as governor. John S. Gibson is interim governor and CEO.
