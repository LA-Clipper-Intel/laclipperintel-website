Cap Terms, Fun-GM Style

Trade Player Exception (TPE)

When a team trades a player away for less salary than it takes back, the difference can become a TPE: room to absorb a player in a later trade without sending matching salary out. The Clippers' $17M Collins TPE is the big one. TPEs expire, so they are a use-it-or-lose-it chip.

Mid-Level Exception (MLE)

A tool that lets an over-the-cap team sign free agents without cap space. The Clippers have $1.0M of it left.

Biannual Exception (BAE)

A smaller exception a team can use only every other year. The Clippers have $5.4M available.

Non-Bird rights

A way to re-sign a team's own free agent, within limits, even when it has no cap space. It is how Bradley Beal's deal was done.

Exhibit 10 contract

A non-guaranteed camp contract. Players who are waived can often be assigned to the team's G League affiliate (for the Clippers, San Diego). That is what happened with Hunter Sallis and Jason Preston on Sept 27.
